Ghumao, chhoo kar dekho —
har roshni ke peechhe ek paighaam chhupa hai.

— tum hi ho is dil mein —

dil ka aakhri raaz

Ab tum ne mere dil ke har kone ko dekh liya hai…

aur har kone ne tumhein ek hi baat kahi hai —

Tum hi ho is dil mein.

Tum hi ho meri dhadkan mein.

Tum hi ho meri mohabbat mein.

Aur shayad…

tum hi ho meri har dua mein.

Ye dil tumhara tha, tumhara hai…
aur tumhara hi rahega.

meri dhadkan taiyaar ho rahi hai…
ek chhupa hua khat · sirf tumhare liye
